Chord editor

Tool
Finger

Click a fret to place a dot (with the selected finger label). Click a dot again to remove it, or with a different finger selected to relabel it. Click above the nut to toggle open (○) / muted (×). Barre tool: click two strings on the same fret. Ctrl/⌘+Z undoes.

Chord library

Voicings are computed for the selected tuning, easiest first. Click one to load it.
